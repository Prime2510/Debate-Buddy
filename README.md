# Debate Buddy

**Debate Buddy** is an AI-powered debate training tool that helps students and debaters strengthen their arguments through intelligent analysis and constructive criticism.

## What It Does

Enter your debate topic and argument, and Debate Buddy will:

- **Challenge Your Position** - Generate strong counter-arguments to test your reasoning
- **Identify Logical Fallacies** - Detect weaknesses in your logic with detailed explanations
- **Provide Strengthening Tips** - Get actionable advice to make your argument more convincing
- **Score Your Argument** - Receive ratings on clarity, logic, evidence, and persuasiveness

## Why Use It?

- **Practice Smarter** - Get instant feedback without waiting for a debate partner
- **Think Critically** - Learn to spot logical flaws before your opponent does
- **Improve Faster** - Iterate on your arguments with AI-powered insights
- **Build Confidence** - Enter debates prepared for the strongest counterpoints

Perfect for debate teams, students preparing for essays or presentations, or anyone looking to sharpen their critical thinking skills.

## Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: HTML, CSS, JavaScript (Vanilla)
- **Backend**: Node.js + Express (proxy server)
- **AI**: Hugging Face API (Mistral-7B-Instruct)
